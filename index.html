What is JAWAD-MD?

JAWAD-MD is a free, open-source WhatsApp bot built by JawadTech. It connects to WhatsApp using a pairing code, so there's no need to scan a QR code or install any extra app. Just enter your number above to get started.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a WhatsApp pairing code?

Enter your WhatsApp number including your country code without '+' or '00', then click 'Generate Pair Code'. Enter the code sent to your phone in WhatsApp Linked Devices section.

Is JAWAD-MD free to use?

Yes! It is completely free and open-source. You can run and manage your bot features 24/7 without extra costs.

What do I do with the pairing code?

Open WhatsApp on your phone, go to Settings > Linked Devices > Link a Device > Link with phone number instead, and paste this code.
